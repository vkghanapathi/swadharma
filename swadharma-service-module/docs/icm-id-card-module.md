# ICM — ID Card Module

Ruled by VKG, 2026-10-08, for CBS, Aayojana and Swadharma alike:

> "Temporary purpose — adhesive labels (such as OPD, Conference Delegates,
> External Invigilators, Visitors); Permanent Associates / Staff / Admin team —
> Plastic Cards (ID Card Module ICM is the name of this program)."

## 1 · What it is

1.1  One module shared across VKG products, not rebuilt per product.
1.2  Two outputs: **plastic photo cards** for people who stay, and **adhesive
     labels** for people who come for a day or an event.
1.3  Status: to be built. Nothing below is serving today.

## 2 · Features for Swadharma tenants

2.1  Plastic photo ID cards for the temple's permanent staff, priests and
     service providers (FSPs) on its roll, and its administration team.
2.2  Adhesive labels for temporary passes — festival and event delegates,
     volunteers for the day, and visitors.
2.3  Character-limit check on every printed field against the card or label
     layout, before printing, so no name or role is cut off.
2.4  Consent of the person to what is printed — name, photograph, role —
     recorded before the print is finalised, and kept with the record.
2.5  A print-ready block — a sheet of labels or a batch of cards — prepared
     for the printer.
