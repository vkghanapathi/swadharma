Purohita · Archaka · Decorator · Cook · Helper · Event Coordinator · Bhokta. Register with Swadharma — not with any one temple — and serve devotees across the institutions you choose.

How this works

1. Fill in the basics below — it takes about three minutes. 2. We verify your phone number. 3. We schedule a short empanelment interview by video call, for each category you wish to be verified in. 4. Once verified you get a profile page on the portal, listed by state and town, carrying your testimonial links.

Verification is per service category — verified for Abhishekam is not verified for Vivāha. You may decline any engagement, at any time, without penalty.
