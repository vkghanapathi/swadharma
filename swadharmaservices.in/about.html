Swadharma Services · Dharma Satram

Module No.7 of the Swadharma suite, under Dharma Poshanam Inc, a 501(c)(3) tax-exempt organisation. Personal rituals, food services and accommodation for pilgrims — a transatlantic paramparā, with scholars in Mysore and centres in Texas and Nebraska.

What Dharma Satram means

A satram is the house that feeds and shelters the pilgrim, asking nothing. The obligation it names has not changed; the circumstances have. A family in Frisco needs a Pārvaṇa Śrāddha performed correctly and has no one within four hundred miles who can do it. A temple in Rajahmundry needs a Ṛtvik team for a Kumbhābhiṣeka. A grandson wants to know which tithi his grandmother's rite falls on.

Swadharma Services is the service layer around those needs: verified professionals, a real catalogue, materials that arrive event-ready, and someone answerable when something is paid for.
