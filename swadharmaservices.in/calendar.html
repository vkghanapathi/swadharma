Pañcāṅga

The monthly programmes, as dates

Every recurring observance is derived from the lunar day rather than kept as a list, so the dates are right for the whole year. Each day shows its tithi and nakṣatra, the muhūrta windows to avoid, and a way to book the rite that belongs to it.

These dates are reckoned for Mysore, India. A tithi begins and ends at a moment in time, so which one stands at sunrise depends on where you are — and sunrise, rāhukāla and durmuhūrta are local outright. Every Swadharma account is set to its own locality when the account is created; this public calendar, and the demo installation, use Mysore.
