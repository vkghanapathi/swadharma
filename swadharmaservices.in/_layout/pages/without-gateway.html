Payments

For organisations without a payment gateway

Last updated 28 October 2026

1. What this is

  1. Many temples, trusts and Vedic institutions conduct ceremonies and sevas but have no online payment gateway of their own.
  2. Swadharma Services lets such an organisation offer its Dharmik services here and take payment for them online.
  3. The payment is taken through the Razorpay account of Sanatana Guru Sampradaya Pratishthanam Trust.
  4. Razorpay settles the organisation's share to the organisation's own bank account weekly (Razorpay Route).
  5. A handling charge of 10%, GST included, is retained and shown as its own line on the receipt.

2. For the devotee or family paying

  1. Each booking names the organisation that conducts the service, and the price it has set.
  2. The receipt names the conducting organisation as payee, and shows the 10% handling charge (GST included) as its own line.
  3. Payments are processed by Razorpay. Card and bank details are entered on Razorpay's page and never reach us.
  4. The service itself — its date, its conduct, its prasādam — is the conducting organisation's responsibility.
  5. Payments are not refunded; see the Cancellation and refund policy.

3. Who this is not for

  1. An organisation with its own Razorpay or Stripe account takes payment directly, through its own Swadharma portal. See For organisations.
  2. Services conducted directly by the Dharma Poshanam group are booked and paid on swadharma.dharmaposhanam.in.
