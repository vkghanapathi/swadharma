Cancellation and refund policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

  1. This policy covers every payment made through Swadharma Services and received by Sanatana Guru Sampradaya Pratishthanam Trust, and every payment taken through its Razorpay account for organisations without a payment gateway.
  2. A seva, service or donation paid once is a single charge. There is nothing to cancel and no further charge is made.
  3. A monthly contribution may be cancelled any time before the next monthly renewal, from the account page or by writing to tech@dharmaposhanam.in. No further charge is made after cancellation.
  4. No refunds. Payments and contributions once made are not refunded, in whole or in part.
  5. No goods are shipped.
  6. At most, the Trust may extend credit instead — added class time or access — at its discretion, never as cash. Access is given online immediately after payment.
