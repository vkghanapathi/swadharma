Cancellation and refund policy

Last updated 24 October 2026

  1. Cancel any time before the next monthly renewal, from the account page or by writing to tech@dharmaposhanam.in. No further charge is made after cancellation.
  2. No refunds. Contributions once made are not refunded, in whole or in part.
  3. No goods are shipped.
  4. At most, the Trust may extend credit instead — added class time or access — at its discretion, never as cash. Access is given online immediately after payment.
