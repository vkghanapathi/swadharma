Jaya Durgā Tīrtham, Undavalli

జయ దుర్గా తీర్థం — అవధూత దత్తపీఠ శాఖ — ధార్మిక క్షేత్రం

A dhārmika kṣetram on the Krishna, a branch of the Avadhūta Datta Pīṭha. Families come here for the rites that want a tīrtha and a riverbank — nadī snāna, tarpaṇa, tīrtha and ābdika śrāddha — and the kṣetram provides the purohita, the materials, the kitchen and the place to sit and eat afterwards.

నదీస్నానాలు, తర్పణాలు, తీర్థశ్రాద్ధాలు, ఆబ్దిక శ్రాద్ధాలు, స్మార్తకర్మలు, వ్రతాలు, శాంతులు, శ్రౌత ఇష్టి కార్యక్రమాలు నిర్వహించుకునే అవకాశం కలదు.

Each opens a service request with the kṣetram's territory already filled in. Tithi-bound rites — an ābdika, a tīrtha śrāddha — can be given by their lunar date rather than a Gregorian one; the request asks either way.

Who may come, and what is arranged

🧑‍🤝‍🧑

Yajamānas, purohitas and archakas

యజమానులు, పురోహితులు, అర్చకులు సంప్రదించగలరు.

A family may come with its own purohita, or ask the kṣetram to appoint one. Purohitas and archakas who wish to conduct rites here are welcome to make arrangements directly.

🍚

Cooking and samārādhana

వంటలు, సమారాధనలు చేసుకోవచ్చును. వంటకు, భోక్తలకు కూడ ఏర్పాటు కలదు.

Cooking and samārādhana can be held on the premises. Arrangements are made both for the cooking itself and for the bhoktas.

Śrī Gaṇapati Sacchidānanda Smārta Āgama Vidyālaya

वेदो नित्यमधीयतां तदुदितं कर्म स्वनुष्ठीयताम्

The kṣetram also carries a vidyālaya — in the light of tradition, for a brighter tomorrow — teaching the paddhati by which these rites are performed rather than only performing them.

Booking through Swadharma

Jaya Durgā Tīrtham is an operational node: Swadharma takes the request, the kṣetram performs the rite. You will be told who is assigned and what it costs before anything is paid, and the kṣetram is reachable directly on the numbers below for anything the form cannot carry.

1

Say what is needed

The rite, the date or the tithi, and how many will attend. No account required.

2

The kṣetram confirms

Purohita, muhūrta, materials, kitchen and seating are settled together rather than as separate errands.

3

The rite is performed

On the bank at Undavalli, and the engagement stays on record for the next observance.
