Terms and conditions

Last updated 28 October 2026

  1. These terms govern the use of Swadharma Services and payments made through it.
  2. Swadharma Services facilitates Dharmik services for organisations that have no payment gateway of their own. Payments for those services are taken through Sanatana Guru Sampradaya Pratishthanam Trust's Razorpay account, and Razorpay settles the organisation's share to the organisation's bank account weekly (Razorpay Route). The organisation receives exactly 90% of the amount, settled to the organisation's own bank account weekly (Razorpay Route). The Trust keeps 10%, and that 10% already includes the gateway charges (GST included); nothing further is deducted. The organisation's 90% is rounded to the nearest paisa (half a paisa rounds up), and the Trust's 10% is the remainder. The receipt is issued by Sanatana Guru Sampradaya Pratishthanam Trust, the integrator through whose gateway the payment is taken. See For organisations without a payment gateway.
  3. The price of each service is set by the organisation that conducts it and is shown before payment.
  4. Payments for Sanskrit Classes are received by Sanatana Guru Sampradaya Pratishthanam Trust as contributions towards the conduct, maintenance and development of the classes.
  5. A one-time payment for a seva or service is a single charge and does not renew. A monthly contribution renews each month until the student cancels. Cancelling stops the next renewal; access continues to the end of the month already paid.
  6. Class allocation is decided by the teachers. Access to lessons is personal to the signed-in student and may not be shared.
  7. Lesson content, recordings and texts remain the property of VKG Foundation and the content's credited owners. They may not be copied or redistributed.
  8. Access may be withdrawn for misuse, after notice to the student.
  9. Payments are processed by Razorpay (India) or Stripe (outside India). We do not see or store card or bank details.
  10. These terms are governed by the laws of India; courts at Mysore have jurisdiction.
  11. We may revise these terms; the date of the current version is shown at the top of the page.
