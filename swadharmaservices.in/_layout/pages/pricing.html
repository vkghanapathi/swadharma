Pricing

Last updated 28 October 2026. Every price is shown before payment, in the currency charged.

1. Dharmik services booked through Swadharma Services

  1. Ceremonies, sevas and other Dharmik services listed on this site are conducted by the organisation named on the booking — a temple, trust or institution.
  2. The price of each service is set by the organisation that conducts it, and is shown on the booking before payment.
  3. Where that organisation has no payment gateway of its own, the payment is taken through Sanatana Guru Sampradaya Pratishthanam Trust's Razorpay account, and Razorpay settles the organisation's share to the organisation's bank account weekly (Razorpay Route).
  4. The organisation receives exactly 90% of the amount, settled to the organisation's own bank account weekly (Razorpay Route). The Trust keeps 10%, and that 10% already includes the gateway charges (GST included); nothing further is deducted. The organisation's 90% is rounded to the nearest paisa (half a paisa rounds up), and the Trust's 10% is the remainder.
  5. The receipt is issued by Sanatana Guru Sampradaya Pratishthanam Trust, the integrator through whose gateway the payment is taken. See For organisations without a payment gateway.
  6. Where the organisation has its own gateway, the payment goes to that organisation directly, and its name is shown at checkout.
  7. Starting a service request on this site charges nothing and collects no payment detail.
