For the staff and pundits

Every assignment says exactly where to go

A seva is performed at the temple or at the devotee's home. The staff and pundits attached to your temple see which — and when it is a home visit, the address and a map link travel with the assignment.

No more telephoning the office to ask for a house number. The purohita, the cook and the helper each open their schedule and find the address already there.

  • At the templeThe assignment carries the kshetram and its address. No devotee address is shown at all.
  • At the homeThe service address is captured at booking and cannot be left blank — an assignment can never ship undispatchable.
  • On the mapOne tap opens the location in Google Maps, with directions from wherever the provider is.
  • Kept privateA devotee's address is visible only to the assigned provider and the office — never in a list, never to other staff.

On the roadmap

Seek a Service — on a map

Choose the seva from a dropdown and see where it can be performed and who is free to perform it: temples offering that seva, and qualified purohitas, cooks and helpers available in the devotee's area.

Temple platforms almost universally present a static list and leave the devotee to work out the rest by telephone. Swadharma turns discovery around — availability becomes something you can see, the way you see a cab on a map.

Availability comes from the same slot engine that drives booking, so what appears on the map is genuinely bookable, not merely present. Providers publish a service area; a provider's home address is never plotted.
