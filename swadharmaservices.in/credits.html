Credits

Who makes this teaching, and on what terms it is offered.

Content & authorship

The teaching material is created by Dr. Vaṁśīkṛṣṇa Ghanapāṭhī. Intellectual property in that material rests with him and with the VKG Foundation.

Where a lesson, translation or commentary is the work of another hand, that hand is named with it.

Scholars

Individual scholars contribute their learning to these classes. They teach here in their own right, not as employees, and their scholarship remains their own.

Institutions

A number of organisations will be named here as they join. The list is expected to grow; its absence of a name today implies nothing about that organisation.

The basis of it

Everyone contributing does so on a non-exclusive basis, for the common cause of learning. No contributor is bound to this platform, and none gives up the right to teach, publish or collaborate elsewhere.

What is shared here is shared so that the subject continues to be learned — that is the whole of the arrangement.

Corrections and additions to this page are welcome. If your work appears here without your name, or your name appears where it should not, write to dps@dharmaposhanam.in and it will be put right.
