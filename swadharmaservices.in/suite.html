Swadharma Services is one door onto a suite of modules built for dharmik life and for the institutions that carry it — a pāṭhaśālā’s accounts, a satram’s kitchen, a family’s muhūrta, a temple’s hiring, a pilgrim’s stay.

Each module stands on its own and can be used on its own. What they share is one register of people, one calendar and one set of dharmik conventions, so a temple that already works with us does not start again with each new need.
